In This Universe, I Found You ✨

For Hades, when you're ready.

To my dearest Hades,

I’ve read your letter, and I honestly don’t know how to begin putting everything I’m feeling into words.

Perhaps because there are things that even someone who loves writing will struggle to articulate. And perhaps because this time, the words carry a weight I wish neither of us had to bear.

First, thank you.

Thank you for trusting me with something so deeply personal. Thank you for allowing yourself to be vulnerable, even when I know vulnerability doesn’t always come easily to you. And thank you for being honest, despite knowing how complicated everything is.

I wish I could tell you that I didn’t see any of this coming. But somewhere along the way, I think a part of me began to wonder. There were little things, little moments, little words that made me question whether there was something more beneath the surface.

And whenever that thought crossed my mind, I hesitated to confront it.

Not because I was afraid of you or what you felt.

I was afraid for you.

Because I know you, Hades. Or at least, I know enough of you to understand how much your principles, your responsibilities, and the people you’ve made commitments to mean to you.

I know this isn’t easy. I know that admitting these feelings must have taken more courage than you probably realize. And I can only imagine how many times you’ve questioned yourself, how many thoughts you’ve had to wrestle with, and how much guilt you might have carried before finally finding the words to tell me.

And it hurts to think that someone I care about so deeply might be suffering in silence.

If I could take even a little of that heaviness away from you, I would.

Hades, please listen to me, I don’t think you’re a bad person for having feelings you never intended to have.

I know you hold yourself to such high standards. I know how much you value doing what is right, even when doing so comes at a personal cost. And I hope you remember that being human means sometimes feeling things that don’t fit neatly into the lives we’ve already built.

What matters is how we choose to carry those feelings and how we treat the people whose lives are intertwined with ours.

And I hope you will give yourself some grace as you navigate all of this.

You don’t have to punish yourself for being human. You don’t have to turn something beautiful into another reason to hate yourself. And you certainly don’t have to carry the weight of this alone.

You know, when I first called you my anam cara, I meant every word.

I have always treasured the kind of connection we share. The honesty, the laughter, the ridiculous conversations, the moments when we can simply be ourselves without having to explain too much.

I’ve always understood that the world we live in has its own boundaries, and that perhaps our paths were never meant to lead us into a romantic relationship.

But that never made what we have any less beautiful to me.

I don't need you to become my romantic partner to know that you were someone extraordinary in my life.

You were already my Hades.

My yellow.

My anam cara.

And that was already more than enough.

But I want to tell you something about what you wrote.

You said that perhaps, in another life, you would find me earlier. That in another universe, things might have been different.

And Hades, if the universe is ever kind enough to give us another lifetime, I hope you find me earlier, too.

I hope that somewhere, in some version of this vast and incomprehensible universe, we meet without the weight of circumstances holding us back.

Where there are no promises to break, no hearts to wound, no duties pulling us in opposite directions. Where we have the liberty to fall in love with each other, freely and without fear. Where choosing each other wouldn’t mean losing something else.

And perhaps, in that universe, we wouldn’t have to wonder about the things that could have been.

Perhaps we would simply get to find out.

But in this lifetime, Hades, what we have here is real, too. And I wouldn’t trade the fact that our paths crossed for anything.

I want you to know that nothing about this confession changes how much I treasure you.

You are still my friend. My confidant. My anam cara.

I won’t see you as someone I need to avoid, or someone whose presence has become too complicated to welcome.

You don’t have to be afraid that telling me the truth has cost you your place in my life.

It hasn’t.

And it won’t.

I’ll still be here for you, Hades.

For the ordinary days, for the difficult ones, for the moments when the world feels unbearably heavy, and for the moments when you simply need someone who will listen.

We may have to be mindful of the boundaries that protect the people and commitments in our lives, but that doesn’t mean we have to lose the friendship that brought us here.

And please, don’t ever think that your feelings have made you less honorable in my eyes. I still see the person I have always respected. The person who tries, even when trying is difficult.

You once told me that your greatest fear was being forgotten.

So let me remind you of something.

You have already become part of the stories I will carry with me.

There will always be conversations I’ll remember because of you, laughter that will remind me of you, and little pieces of yellow that will make me think of the person who made my world a little warmer.

And no matter how many chapters life has waiting for us, I will always be grateful that one of them had you in it.

Thank you for loving me in the way you know how.

Thank you for seeing me, for believing in me, and for allowing me to see the parts of you that you don’t always show the world.

And please know that even if our love takes a different shape in this lifetime, it doesn’t make it any less sincere.

Not every love has to become a romance to become one of the most meaningful loves of our lives.

If we are meant to be anam cara in this universe, then I will cherish that with all the tenderness and gratitude it deserves.

And if there really are other universes out there, perhaps somewhere among the stars, another Persephone and another Hades are meeting for the very first time.

And perhaps, this time, they are free to fall in love.

But here, in this life, I’m simply grateful that I found you.

And I hope you know that you will always have a friend in me. I will always appreciate you. I will always be glad for your existence.

You will always have me who loves you.

And one last thing, Hades.

Please don’t worry too much about me, okay?

I know you might be wondering how all of this has affected me, whether I’m hurting, or whether there’s something you need to do to make things easier for me.

But I want you to know that I’ll be fine. Really, I will.

You don’t have to carry my feelings on top of everything you’re already trying to make sense of. You don’t have to feel responsible for my happiness, and you certainly don’t have to feel guilty for being honest with me.

I’ve been through things that have taught me how to sit with difficult emotions, how to accept what I cannot change, and how to find peace even when life doesn’t unfold the way I imagined.

And I’ll find my peace here, too.

So please, be gentle with yourself. Take care of your heart, your peace, and the life you’ve built.

I don’t want you to spend your days worrying about whether I’m okay.

Because I am, Hades. And I will be.

I promise.

And if there’s anything I hope you take away from all these words, it’s that you haven’t lost me.

You still have your Persephone.

And I’m going to be okay. 💜

Always and always,

Your Persephone. 💜💛
